Game mood mock

Schematic only — not production sprite art. Used to validate contrast: silver protagonist against navy/teal midnight water.

Animation language

Idle
4 frames

Almost no acting: 1px settle, ear lag, tiny chrome shift. The Chibi stays sleepy and unimpressed.

Hook-smack
7 frames

Neutral → anticipation → violent rod smear → impact → recoil → instantly deadpan again. The body and rod act; the face barely does.
